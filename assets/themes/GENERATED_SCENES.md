# Generated scene backgrounds

> Initial artwork/provenance record. These original roomy backgrounds were subsequently replaced by closer compositions for the 5-inch display, with an apartment added. The saved scene configuration determines the active day/night background files; do not assume the original filenames or prompts below describe current placements. See [day/night authoring](../../docs/day-night-authoring.md).

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool using `cozy_study/background.jpg` as a style reference. Original PNG outputs are preserved as `video_store/background.png` and `resort_beach/background.png`; the application scales them to its 1280×720 canvas.

## Video store prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a new game background: late 1980s / early 1990s video rental store, matching the attached study's detailed SCUMM point-and-click pixel art, warm outlines and perspective. Reference image is style only. Wide 16:9 full scene, no people, no UI. Rows of colourful VHS tapes on back and side shelves, counter and CRT near back right, glass entrance at left, second doorway at back right. Spacious continuous walkable floor across lower half. A low freestanding VHS shelf near lower right with clear routes around it, usable later as a foreground prop. Avoid readable branded text. Crisp pixel-art edges. Entire composition suited to character walking left/right and into depth.

Wide 16:9 landscape resort beach, no people, no sprites, no UI. Palm trees, turquoise ocean and distant horizon, a thatched tiki bar at back right, two deck chairs to the right, generous empty sandy walkable area across lower half suitable for a character later digging. Sandy path leaving at left edge and second beach path leaving right edge. Keep central foreground clear for navigation, no text or logos.
